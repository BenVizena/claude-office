Getting around

  • WASD / arrows — walk (hold Shift to hurry)
  • E — talk to / look at whatever is next to you
  • Click the floor — walk there · Click anything else — open it
  • Right-click furniture — ask the designer about it, move, rotate, resize, duplicate, remove, or let it wander
  • Mouse wheel — zoom
  • M — sound on/off · Esc — close panel
  • 1–9 — jump to a room · 0 — lobby

Reading the office

  • Each room is a project; each desk pod is a worktree, its sign shows the branch.
  • Each character is a Claude Code session. Typing = working, sipping coffee = idle, jacket on the chair = stepped away, ✋ = needs your input.
  • Mugs and energy cans pile up as a session's context fills. Paper stacks = uncommitted files.
  • Dust and cobwebs collect on desks that haven't been touched in days.
  • Talk to the designer by the door to redecorate a room.
